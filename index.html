Standard Engineering Formulas & Sizing Logic

1. Design Current ($I_d$) Calculation

1-Phase: $I_d = \frac{P_{Watts}}{V_{LN} \times \cos\phi}$

3-Phase: $I_d = \frac{P_{Watts}}{\sqrt{3} \times V_{LL} \times \cos\phi}$

2. Breaker Sizing Rule ($I_n$)

According to BNBC/NFPA Article 210, circuit protection must be sized for 125% of continuous load:

$I_n \ge 1.25 \times I_d$

Standard MCB trip ratings selected: 6A, 10A, 16A, 20A, 25A, 32A, 40A, 50A, 63A, 80A, 100A, 125A, 160A, 200A, 250A.

3. Voltage Drop Equation ($\%V_d$)

$\%V_d = \left( \frac{2 \times L \times I_d \times R_c}{V_{LN}} \right) \times 100$

Where $L$ is wire run distance (meters), $R_c$ is conductor resistance ($\Omega/km$), and allowable limit is $\le 3\%$ for branch circuits.

4. Phase Unbalance Percentage

$\% \text{Unbalance} = \frac{|I_{max} - I_{avg}|}{I_{avg}} \times 100\%$
